## Content Writing Tips

### Mission Brief (2-3 sentences):
- Focus on OUTCOME, not process
- Use specific numbers when possible
- Make it sound impressive but honest
- Example: "Build X that handles Y with Z performance"

### Challenge Section:
- What was broken/missing/slow?
- Why did it matter?
- What was at stake?
- Keep it relatable

### Solution Section:
- High-level technical approach
- Key architectural decisions
- Why this approach?
- 2-3 sentences max

### Impact Section:
- Quantifiable results (%, $, time saved, users gained)
- Business outcomes, not just technical
- Before/After comparison
- Example: "Reduced deployment time from 4 hours to 8 minutes"

### Tech Stack:
- Be specific but not overwhelming
- Group related technologies
- 5-8 technologies max per project
- Put the most impressive/relevant first

---

## Examples of Great Mission Briefs

❌ Bad: "Built an e-commerce website using React and Node.js"
✅ Good: "Built a next-generation e-commerce platform handling 1M+ concurrent users with sub-100ms response times"

❌ Bad: "Created a dashboard for analytics"
✅ Good: "Real-time analytics pipeline processing 50M events/day with <500ms latency"

❌ Bad: "Made a component library"
✅ Good: "Design system used by 200+ developers across 15 products, reducing development time by 60%"
